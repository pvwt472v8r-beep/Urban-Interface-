Case study · Small business website redesign

Peak Landscapes LLC

A ground-up redesign for a Colorado Front Range landscaping company. We rebuilt the visual design, page structure, and inquiry flow around how homeowners actually choose a landscaper: find the right service, see the quality of the work, and know how to get an estimate.

Visit Peak Landscapes All case studies

From browsing to requesting an estimate.

  1. 01

    Design around the homeowner’s decision

    A retaining wall, a water-wise yard, and a new outdoor living space lead to very different questions. Rather than ask every visitor to read the same long pitch, we organized the site around the decisions they share: whether Peak offers the right service, whether it works in their area, and whether its past projects match what they have in mind.

  2. 02

    Rebuild the interface from the ground up

    We gave the homepage a new visual hierarchy, pairing a Colorado-focused headline with clear paths to services and project photos. A dedicated Services page makes distinct work, from drainage and retaining walls to xeriscaping, easier to scan. The gallery gives Peak’s projects room to speak for themselves instead of burying the photos in service copy.

  3. 03

    Make the next step fit the visitor

    Someone gathering ideas can go straight to the gallery. A homeowner with a specific job can start with Services. Anyone ready to talk can call or send an estimate request. The redesign gives each visitor a useful next step without forcing everyone through the same path or asking them to contact Peak before they’re ready.
