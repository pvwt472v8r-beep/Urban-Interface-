Case study · Small business website redesign

Peak Landscapes LLC

A full website overhaul for a Colorado Front Range landscaping company, built around three things homeowners want to do: understand the services, see the work, and request a free estimate.

Visit Peak Landscapes Back to Work

From browsing to requesting an estimate.

  1. 01

    What needed to change

    The site needed to explain a wide range of services without making homeowners hunt for the basics: what Peak does, where it works, and how to get a quote.

  2. 02

    What we built

    We rebuilt the homepage and gave services, project photos, and contact information dedicated pages. The homepage introduces everything from landscape installation and retaining walls to water-wise xeriscaping and outdoor living.

  3. 03

    What visitors can do now

    Homeowners can check whether Peak serves their area, compare services, browse real project photos, and call or send a request for a free estimate.
