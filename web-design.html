Before you get started

Common questions.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We'll look at what's already working, what visitors struggle to find, and what should carry over before deciding what to rebuild.

What if my content or photos aren't ready?

We can start with the material you have and identify what you still need, such as project photos, service descriptions, or a team introduction.

What affects the cost and timeline?

The number of pages, the features you need, and how much content is ready all affect the scope. We'll discuss those details before you commit.
